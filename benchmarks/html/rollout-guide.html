Rollout guide

Staged release for the queue worker.

Before widening traffic

  • Send 5% of jobs to the new worker.
  • Watch job timeouts for 15 minutes.
  • Widen traffic if the error rate stays below 1%.
Rollback steps
  1. Route all jobs to the previous worker.
  2. Wait for the new worker to drain.
  3. Record the failed release in the incident log.
